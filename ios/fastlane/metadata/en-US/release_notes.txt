Course Smash now opens on Today. A short daily recall brings back questions from what you have already learned, and answering 5 in a day keeps your streak. When recall is done, one tap carries on with your course.

The app has four tabs: Today, City, Library and You. You has your streak, the last 4 weeks, your stats, your daily goal and Log out. Tap the streak at the top of Today, City, Library or a course to see it in full.
