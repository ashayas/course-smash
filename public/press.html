In a sentence

Course Smash turns what you want to learn into a course of questions, brings it back a few questions a day so you keep it, and grows a city as you go.

In a paragraph

Say what you want to learn, in a sentence. Course Smash plans a short course and teaches it entirely through questions: you answer, it tells you what you got right and where your thinking went off, and the next questions follow how you answered. Every course goes up as a building in your own isometric city, one floor per module, and a floor's windows light up when you pass it. Once you've passed a module, its ideas come back in a daily recall of about ten questions, sooner if you missed them and later if you got them right. Answer five questions in a day and the day counts toward your streak. There is also a library of ready-made courses on AI engineering, Kubernetes, inference, identity, cybersecurity, robotics and AI policy, grouped into learning paths.

Facts

  • Where it runs. On the web at coursesmash.app. Apps for iPhone and Android are in testing as of November 2026 and are not in the stores yet.
  • Accounts. By invitation for now. There is no sign-up screen.
  • How it grades. Multiple choice is checked against an answer key. Short answers are graded by an AI model on what you mean, not on matching words.
  • What it costs. There are no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • Who makes it. Ashaya Sharma. The code is open at github.com/ashayas/course-smash.
