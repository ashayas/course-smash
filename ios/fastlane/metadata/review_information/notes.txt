Course Smash needs a login. The app has no sign-up: accounts are created by the server's operator, so there is no account creation or in-app purchase to review.

Use the demo account in the sign-in fields of this submission.

What to try:
1. Log in.
2. Open "Library", pick any course and tap "Start this course". Answer a few questions.
3. On the course screen tap "Change building" to restyle the course's building.
4. Open "Stats" to see answers per day and scores.
5. On "Courses", type what you want to learn and tap "Build my course". Planning takes up to a minute.

Short answers are graded by an AI model on the server. Multiple choice answers are graded by an answer key.
