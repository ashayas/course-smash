Common questions

How do I get an account?

Accounts are set up by whoever runs the Course Smash server you use. The app has no sign-up screen.

I forgot my password

Ask the person who set up your account to give you a new one.

How do I delete a course?

Open the course and choose "Delete course". Its questions and your answers are deleted with it.

How do I delete my account?

Ask through one of the links above and say which username it is. The account, its courses and its answers are then removed.

A question was graded wrongly

Short answers are graded by an AI model, and it can be wrong. Keep going: later questions come back to the same idea, and your score moves with them.
