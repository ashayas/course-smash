Course Smash

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. This covers the Course Smash website at coursesmash.app and the Course Smash app for iPhone.

The short version

Course Smash keeps what it needs to teach you: your login, the courses you start and the answers you give. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What is stored

  • Your account. A username and a salted hash of your password. The password itself is never stored.
  • Your courses. What you say you want to learn, the course outline and questions written for you, and the building you pick for each course.
  • Your answers. What you answer, how each answer was graded, and the feedback you were given. Your stats are worked out from these.

All of it lives in a Cloudflare D1 database that belongs to Course Smash.

How it is used

Your learning goal and your answers are sent to AI models to write your questions, grade short answers and write feedback. The models run on Cloudflare Workers AI. They get the text of the goal, question and answer, and not your username or password.

Cloudflare hosts the service and keeps short-lived technical logs of requests, such as the time, the address requested and your IP address, to keep the service running and to limit repeated login attempts.

On your device

  • iOS app. After you log in, the app keeps a session token in the iOS Keychain so you stay signed in. Logging out deletes it. The app asks for no permissions, and uses no advertising identifier and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Website. One cookie keeps you signed in for up to 14 days. There are no other cookies. The site loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google sees that request.

Keeping and deleting

Deleting a course in the app or on the website deletes its questions and your answers to them straight away. Your data is kept until you delete it or ask for your account to be removed. To have your account and everything in it deleted, ask through the support page.

Children

Course Smash is not aimed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly given to them.
