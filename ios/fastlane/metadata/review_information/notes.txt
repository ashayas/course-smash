Course Smash needs a login. The app has no sign-up: accounts are created by the server's operator, so there is no account creation or in-app purchase to review.

Use the demo account in the sign-in fields of this submission.

What to try:
1. Log in. The app opens on "Today".
2. If Today shows a "Daily recall" question, answer it and a few more, then tap the close button at the top left. A new account has nothing to recall yet, and Today shows the course to continue instead.
3. Open "Library", pick any course and tap "Start this course". Answer a few questions.
4. On the course screen tap "Change building" to restyle the course's building.
5. Open "You" to see the streak and change the daily goal, then tap "Stats" to see answers per day and scores.
6. On "City", type what you want to learn and tap "Build my course". Planning takes up to a minute.
7. "Log out" is at the bottom of "You".

Short answers are graded by an AI model on the server. Multiple choice answers are graded by an answer key.
