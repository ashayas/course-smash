Say what you want to learn. Course Smash builds a course of questions, reads how you answer, and changes what comes next.

Every course goes up as a building in your city, one floor per module. Pass a module and its floor lights up.

HOW IT WORKS
• Describe what you want to understand and why, in a sentence. An AI agent plans a short course around it.
• Answer multiple choice and short answer questions. Short answers are graded on what you mean, not on matching words.
• Get direct feedback on each answer, with the idea to remember.
• Miss something and the next questions come back to it. Know it already and the course moves on.

YOUR CITY
• Each course is its own isometric building: a lighthouse for observability, a fortress for security, a greenhouse for biology.
• Floors are modules. Windows light up as you pass them.
• Change any building's shape, material, roof and trim colour.

THE LIBRARY
• Ready-made courses on AI engineering, Kubernetes, inference, identity, cybersecurity, robotics and AI policy.
• Each module has a bank of questions behind it, so follow-ups target what you missed.
• Tell a library course what you want out of it, and it sets how well you need to know each module.

YOUR STATS
• Answers per day for the last 30 days, your streak and your average score.
• For each course: the modules you passed, what you are strongest on and what needs work.

Course Smash needs an account on a Course Smash server. The app has no sign-up screen: accounts are set up by whoever runs the server you use.

No ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
